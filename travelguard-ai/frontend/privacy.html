Privacy Policy

TravelGuard AI collects only what it needs to run your account:

  • Your username, used to identify your account.
  • A securely hashed version of your password. Plaintext passwords are never stored.
  • Your saved travel plans and assistance history, which stay private to your account.

We do not sell personal data. Session cookies are used to keep you logged in and are never used for advertising. API keys and secrets are kept on the server and never exposed in the browser.
