Safety Guidance

Medical Emergency

  • Call emergency services immediately
  • Do not move injured person unless in danger
  • Carry ID, insurance, and medication list
  • Ask for English-speaking staff if needed

Lost Documents

  • Report to local police immediately
  • Contact your embassy/consulate
  • Get a police report for insurance
  • Keep digital copies of documents

Safety Concern

  • Trust your instincts — leave unsafe areas
  • Share location with trusted contacts
  • Know your embassy's emergency contact
  • Register with your country's travel program

Travel Insurance

  • Contact insurer before treatment if possible
  • Keep all receipts and medical reports
  • Know your policy's emergency number
  • Check coverage for medical evacuation
